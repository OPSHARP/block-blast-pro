2. OVERVIEW OF OUR PRIVACY PRINCIPLES
- We do not require you to create an account or provide personal details such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number to play the game.
- We do not operate external cloud servers or databases that store your individual identity or gameplay data.
- Game progress, high scores, unlocked skins, daily streak progression, and user settings are saved locally on your device storage.
- We integrate trusted third-party advertising and payment SDKs (Google AdMob, Unity Ads, and Google Play Billing) to provide advertisements, reward features, and optional in-app subscriptions.


3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AND HOW IT IS COLLECTED

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
Block Blast Pro stores certain gameplay data directly on your device using local storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device through operating system backups. Local data includes:
- High scores, total points, and level records.
- Game settings such as sound volume, music toggles, and haptic feedback preferences.
- In-game inventory and unlocks, including custom block skins (Neon Cyberpunk, Woodcraft, Jewel Prism, Obsidian Glow).
- Daily login streaks and available power-ups or reroll tokens.
- Ad-removal entitlement flags verified through Google Play Billing.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services
When you play Block Blast Pro, certain third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the game may automatically collect technical and device-related information. This information is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and process in-app transactions. Such information may include:
- Device identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID or GAID), device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, and system language.
- Network and connectivity information: IP address, mobile network carrier, connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular), and general geographic location derived from IP address (such as country or city level).
- Performance and diagnostic data: App crash logs, load times, frame rate metrics, and SDK interaction events.
- Ad interaction data: Impressions, clicks, video completion status, and ad unit engagement.

C. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your real name, home address, personal phone number, or contact list.
- We do not access your device camera, microphone, photos, media gallery, or biometric sensors.
- We do not collect precise GPS location data.
- We do not collect or store credit card, debit card, or banking details. All payment processing is handled solely and securely by Google Play Billing.

5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS USED BY BLOCK BLAST PRO
Block Blast Pro requests only the minimal set of permissions required for normal operation:
- INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Needed to retrieve ad content from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, perform Google Play license checks, and facilitate in-app updates.
- ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Allows the app to verify whether an active network connection exists before attempting ad or purchase requests.
- BILLING (com.android.vending.BILLING): Required to process optional in-app subscriptions and purchases securely through Google Play.
- AD_ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Allows ad networks to access the Google Advertising ID on devices running Android 12 and above, subject to your system-level ad privacy settings.


6. YOUR CHOICES AND HOW TO CONTROL YOUR DATA

A. Resetting or Deleting Your Advertising ID
You can control personalized advertising or reset/delete your Android Advertising ID at any time directly through your device settings:
- On Android 12 or newer: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads. You can tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
- On older Android versions: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and toggle on "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

B. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising
When you opt out of personalized ads or delete your Advertising ID, you will still see advertisements in Block Blast Pro, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your browsing interests.

C. Managing In-App Subscriptions
You can view, cancel, or modify your "Remove Ads" subscription at any time by opening the Google Play Store app, tapping your profile icon in the top right, selecting "Payments and subscriptions", and choosing "Subscriptions".

D. Clearing Local Game Data
Because your high scores, streak records, and unlocked skins are saved locally on your device, you can completely erase all local data by going to Android Settings > Apps > Block Blast Pro > Storage > Clear Data. Uninstalling the app also deletes this local data unless saved by an automated operating system cloud backup.
